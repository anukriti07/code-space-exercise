# OctoAcme Project Management Process Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation. These guides define how we run projects, deliver customer value, and coordinate work across product, engineering, QA, and leadership. OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative delivery model built around clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning.

## Overview

OctoAcme's project management approach is designed to keep teams aligned while staying responsive to customer feedback and changing priorities. Our core principles are:

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value, usability, and business impact
- **Iterative delivery**: Break work into small, testable increments that can be shipped and validated quickly
- **Clear ownership**: Every initiative has explicit accountability for outcomes, decisions, and follow-through
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure progress, risk, and impact using evidence and feedback
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage honest communication, learning, and healthy challenge

The project lifecycle is designed to move from concept to value delivery in a repeatable, transparent way. Each phase has clear expectations, outcomes, and communication practices that help teams stay focused and aligned.

## Process Lifecycle Summary

Every OctoAcme project follows a common lifecycle:

### 1. Initiation
- Validate the business need and customer problem
- Confirm goals, constraints, and success criteria
- Align stakeholders and authorize the project

### 2. Planning
- Define scope, milestones, and dependencies
- Break work into shippable increments
- Identify risks, owners, and communication expectations

### 3. Execution and Tracking
- Build and validate work in progress
- Manage daily progress, blockers, and quality checks
- Review progress against milestones and objectives

### 4. Release and Deployment
- Prepare the release scope, validation steps, and rollback strategy
- Coordinate deployment readiness and stakeholder communication
- Confirm go-live readiness and monitor outcomes

### 5. Retrospective and Continuous Improvement
- Capture lessons learned and identify improvement opportunities
- Convert insights into actionable follow-up items
- Improve the process for future projects

This lifecycle ensures that work is planned, delivered, and refined in a consistent way while preserving flexibility where needed.

## Quick Reference

### Roles
- **Product Manager (PM)**: Owns delivery outcomes, stakeholder alignment, and prioritization
- **Product Designer / PdM**: Guides product direction, customer experience, and usability
- **Developers**: Build, test, and deliver increments of value
- **QA / Quality Engineers**: Validate quality, identify risks, and support release readiness

### Key Artifacts
- Project charter or initiation brief
- Scope and milestone plan
- Backlog or work plan
- Risk register
- Status updates and stakeholder communication notes
- Release checklist and deployment notes
- Retrospective actions and improvement log

### Communication Cadence
- Project kickoff and alignment at initiation
- Planning and milestone reviews during delivery
- Weekly or recurring status updates for stakeholders
- Risk escalation when issues threaten delivery or customer impact
- Post-release review and retrospective after major milestones or launches
